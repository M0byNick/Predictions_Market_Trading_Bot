Settle (locked) = pair-level cash flow locked at entry; for a correctly-paired arb this is deterministic at expiry regardless of outcome. This is the right adjudication metric — negative means the pair entered at unfavorable prices (phantom-edged) and should be deactivated.

Mid-mtm = mark-to-(bid+ask)/2; reflects current market re-pricing of the position. Exec close = close longs at bid + shorts at ask; the gap from mid is the bid-ask liquidity tax. Both move with bid/ask volatility but DON'T affect what we collect at settlement.
